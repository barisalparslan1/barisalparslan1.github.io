Differentially Private Distributed Bayesian Linear Regression with MCMC

Year: 2023

We introduce a Bayesian framework for distributed differentially private linear regression using noisy shared summary statistics. A novel generative model enables efficient MCMC-based inference and a fast one-step approximation, achieving strong estimation and prediction performance with computational advantages over existing methods.

Statistic Selection and MCMC for Differentially Private Bayesian Estimation

Year: 2022

This paper studies differentially private Bayesian estimation using noisy shared sample statistics, focusing on which statistics should be privatized utilizing Fisher information and how to perform effective inference. The paper shows that optimal statistics under privacy differ from non-private settings and proposes tailored MCMC methods for accurate posterior inference.
